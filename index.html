Window: FY2025-26 (2025-01 → 2026-11). Reliable per-ton pricing begins FY2025 (2023–24 rebar quantity entries are mixed-unit).

Method

  • Realised price = net value ÷ quantity, per selling unit as booked (MT).
  • Month-on-month price compares to the previous month with sales for that SKU.
  • Elasticity = slope of ln(volume) on ln(realised price), log-log OLS. Reported when ≥18 valid months and |β| ≤ 8.
  • Volume = net revenue ÷ realised price (implied MT), so it stays consistent with the booked unit.
  • Realised ÷ MRP outside 0.35–1.6 flags a unit mismatch (not reconciled to declared pack).
